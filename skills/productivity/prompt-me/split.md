# Split the work

**Pack** the work into the fewest passes that fit their budgets: extend each pass with the next steps until one more would exceed its budget, then cut at the nearest seam between context-heavy working sets. Keep steps that share evidence in one pass. Each pass produces a verifiable result, and explicit handoff artifacts carry it to later passes.

List each pass in the brief's plan with these fields:

- **Estimate:** its itemized estimate against its budget
- **Deliverable:** the result it produces
- **Prerequisites:** the inputs and earlier outputs it needs
- **Check:** observable proof that it is done

**Done when:** every pass fits its budget and every merge of adjacent passes would exceed it.
