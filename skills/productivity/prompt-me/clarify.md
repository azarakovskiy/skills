# Clarify in rounds

The **frontier** is every open conceptual decision whose prerequisites are settled. A decision that depends on an unanswered question or a pending lookup waits for a later round.

Ask the whole frontier in one message, numbered, each with a recommended answer that names an outcome or limit:

```text
❓ **Q1** - **<question title>**: <question body>

➡️ <recommended answer>
```

Separate entries with `---` on its own line, with blank lines around it.

Wait for the answers, fold them in, and recompute the frontier.

**Done when:** the frontier is empty; continue with the brief.
