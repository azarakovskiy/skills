# Present the decision

Lead with the conclusion in two levels, scaled to requested depth, language and style (ASD-STE100 when requested):

1. **Decision:** best course for this user—buy, keep or wait—or best chosen-product offer. Explain personal benefit, total cost and compromise. Include alternatives only for a distinct personal reason, e.g. a cheaper adequate fit or meaningful stretch. Show where extra spending stops helping.
2. **Support, on demand or at requested depth:** relevant technical facts and meaning in use, material cost/compatibility caveats and specific sources. Distinguish evidence, judgement and uncertainty; state any condition and named next check the advice depends on. Keep the full audit trail in the research note.

Synthesize first. Create one final artifact only when requested, in the requested form. Lead with the decision and reveal support progressively, not a research catalogue.
